# Contributing to FOUNDRY

Thank you for your interest in improving FOUNDRY!

### The "Inbound=Outbound" Policy
By submitting a Pull Request, you agree that your contribution is licensed under the project's existing [Foundry Source License (FSL) v1.0](LICENSE). 

You retain your copyright, but you grant FOUNDRY SYSTEMS and all users the right to use your contribution under the terms of the FSL.

### How to Contribute
1. **Fork** the repository.
2. **Clone** your fork locally.
3. **Create a branch** for your feature or bugfix.
4. **Commit** your changes.
5. **Push** to your fork and submit a **Pull Request**.

### Code Style
* Keep it "Zero-Dependency" where possible.
* Maintain the "Industrial/Technical" aesthetic.
* Ensure all new features work with the local SQLite database.
